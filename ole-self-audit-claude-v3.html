Le prompt d'audit (copie-colle)

Ouvre une session Claude et colle ce prompt. Claude lit tout ton setup et te dit exactement quoi couper.

claude — audit CLAUDE.md
À copier
Audit my entire Claude setup. Read every CLAUDE.md, every skill, and every context file. For each rule or instruction, check: (1) Does the model already do this by default? (2) Does it conflict with another rule? (3) Is it too vague to act on? (4) Is it a duplicate? (5) Is it still relevant? Flag everything that fails any filter. Tell me exactly what to cut and why.
Cowork ou Claude.ai : fonctionne dans n'importe quelle session Claude avec accès à tes fichiers de configuration.
